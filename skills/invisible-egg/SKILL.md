# Invisible Egg (L'œuf invisible)

Chapter 0 of the Butterfly Process. Honor the fertile void before structure.

## When to use

- New venture, no clear problem statement yet
- User wants to "define the MVP" too early
- Creative block or fear of the void

## Principles

- Intuition before plan; vibration before structure
- Do not frame target, MVP, or business model yet — they suffocate the signal
- Dissolution is productive (caterpillar → chrysalis metaphor)
- Collect weak signals without filtering

## Workflow

1. **Hold the space** — acknowledge discomfort; frame flou as terrain de jeu
2. **Imaginals journal** — capture images, sensations, recurring obsessions, body reactions (no triage)
3. **Resist framing reflexes** — redirect requests for personas, roadmap, or pitch deck to Chapter 1–2
4. **Name fears** — vide, contrôle, irrationalité as initiatory allies

## Output

- Imaginals journal entries (raw, unfiltered)
- Short reflection on what keeps returning (no solution yet)

## Ritual

**Journal des Imaginaux**: physical or digital sanctuary for sparks — observe, do not develop yet.
