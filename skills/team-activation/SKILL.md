# Team Activation

Chapters 8–11. Shared compass, systemic energy, product team as converter.

## Shared language

Give each member:

- Where the project sits in the Process
- Their role in current loop
- How to dialogue across loops without friction

## Organizational poles

| Pole | Teams | Feeds |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Input/Income** | Marketing, sales, support, comms, partners | Sense loop — signals, revenue, visibility |
| **Output/Outcome** | Design, dev, ops, engineering | Incarnation loop — tangible output, user outcome |
| **Bridge** | Product team | Breathes between both |

Revalorize "peripheral" functions — support and sales are **core sensors**, not afterthoughts.

## Facilitator posture

- Maintain step clarity (not control)
- Enable contribution at each level
- Reactivate listening and reformulation
- Orchestrate symmetry: real inputs → relevant outputs

Roles: PM, coach, designer, founder — posture matters more than title.

## Product team rituals

**Sense loop**

- Sensitive user returns
- Field observation sessions
- Problem reframing workshops

**Incarnation loop**

- Rapid prototype sprints
- Frequent qual tests
- Cross-functional alignment points

## Peripheral teams (Chapter 9)

| Function | Contribution |
|----------|--------------|
| Sales | Verbatims, appetite tests, co-created pitches |
| Marketing | Narrative definition, test content, emotional impact |
| Support | Usage friction patterns, ticket-driven tensions |

## PM as dancer

- Nourish vision with recent learning
- Identify leverage priorities
- Dialogue across business, design, tech, users
- Hold course without rigidity

## Strategic listening

Develop **collective informed intuition** — not yes to everything, not numbers-only validation.

## Output

- Team loop placement (who is where now)
- Ritual calendar per loop
- Input → tension → output → outcome trace for current priority
