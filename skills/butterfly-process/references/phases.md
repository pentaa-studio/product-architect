## Input / Income ↔ Output / Outcome

| Pole | Teams | Role in Process |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Input/Income | Marketing, sales, support, comms | Feed the sense loop |
| Output/Outcome | Design, dev, ops, engineering | Feed the incarnation loop |
| Bridge | Product team | Breathe between both loops |
