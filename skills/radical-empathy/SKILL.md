# Radical Empathy

Chapter 2. Listen to be transformed, not to confirm hypotheses.

## Mindset

- Come without filter, agenda, or pre-set strategy
- Seek what traverses people, not what they "want" as features
- Listening is political: do not impose your vision

## Techniques

| Technique | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Open interviews | Open questions; follow emotion, metaphor, contradiction |
| Passive observation | Gestures, hesitations, workarounds — what they do, not say |
| Immersion | Live the user's context (support day, competitor product, workshop) |
| Field journal | Daily notes on behavior, weak signals, cross-patterns |

## What to capture

**Tensions:** where it rubs, compensations, self-deception, adaptation instead of authenticity

**Positive signals:** unconscious smiles, repeated habits, unasked needs

## Interview discipline

- Use a thread to stay on topic, but let the person lead direction
- Hunt for hypotheses you would never have thought alone
- Do not validate your search-feature assumption — listen for cognitive/emotional tension

## Closing ritual

Answer in one plain sentence:

> *Quelle est la tension principale que j'ai ressentie dans cette rencontre avec l'autre ?*

## Output

- Field notes / verbatims
- **One tension statement** (not a solution, not a feature)
- Evidence you were moved or displaced — if not, listen deeper

## Pitfalls

- Listening to confirm
- Monetizing insights too fast
- Projecting yourself as the user
