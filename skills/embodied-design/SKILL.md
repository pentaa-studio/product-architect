# Embodied Design

Chapter 5. Form intelligence in service of meaning — not decoration.

## Core questions

- What should the person **feel** at contact?
- What **posture** should they adopt (guided museum vs free forest walk)?
- What **emotion** remains after?

## Design = relation

- Reveals intention; does not hide it
- Story arc per journey: introduction → tension → resolution
- Respect time, attention, perception — invite, don't shout

## Collaboration

Balance fragile equilibrium between:

- Marketing → clarity
- Engineering → simplicity
- Design → coherence

Power emerges from **contradictory feedback**, not single-voice polish.

## Anti-pattern

Over-polish kills the soul — preserve initial vibration in final form.

## Workflow

1. Anchor on tension + HMW from Chapter 3
2. Define emotional intent per key journey
3. Map story arc for primary flows
4. Detail only what reinforces rhythm and intention
5. Cross-check with prototype learnings

## Output

- Experience principles (3–5 bullets)
- Journey story arc for primary flow
- Design decisions linked to tension (traceability)
