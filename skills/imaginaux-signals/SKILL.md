# Imaginaux — Weak Signals

Chapter 1. Recognize imaginaux before they become structured ideas.

## Signals to capture

- Obsessive phrases, looping images
- Body reactions (frisson, tension, lightness)
- Recurring emotions (anger at injustice, unexplained sadness, vivid joy)
- Repetitions in environment (same book, overheard conversation, recurring word)

## Workflow

1. **Receive** — no judgment, no business translation
2. **Log** in intuition journal (can be poetic, discontinuous)
3. **Cluster** — link entries that resonate across time
4. **Follow excitement** — map associations without requiring coherence
5. **Stop before framing** — do not name the product yet

## Anti-patterns

- Dismissing as distraction or "not serious"
- Premature naming, ICP, or feature list
- Forcing logical narrative too early

## Output

- Intuition journal (dated entries)
- Optional: archipelago map (word/image associations)
- 3–5 signals that insist most — still no solution
